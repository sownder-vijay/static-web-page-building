# Connect2Home

Connect2Home is a reliable, long-distance lunch delivery management system built for students and working professionals who live far from home. The platform manages complex delivery routes to ensure that fresh, home-cooked food reaches you right on time, no matter how far away you are working or studying.

## Key Features

* **Long-Distance Routing:** Smart route management optimized for delivering food across long distances between homes and workplaces/campuses.
* **On-Time Delivery:** Focuses on strict schedules so users get their lunch exactly when they need it.
* **Multi-User Platform:** Designed to support school/college students, corporate employees, and working professionals.
* **Freshness Tracking:** Ensures delivery timelines are met so the food stays fresh.

## 👥 Target Audience

* **Working Professionals:** Employees who want real home-cooked meals at their offices instead of eating out.
* **Students:** School and college students living in hostels or rooms far away from their families.
* **Home Chefs / Kitchens:** The network of homes preparing the food.
